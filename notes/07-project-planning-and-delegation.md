# 07. Project planning and Delegation (exercise lesson, no video)

Estimated time: 20 min

## Learning objective
- Apply Delegation to a practical **multi-step project** that you carry through the rest of the course (it is reused in Description, Discernment and Diligence lessons).

## Step 1: Choose your project
It should be:
- **Substantial** enough to involve multiple types of task
- **Manageable** within about **1 hour** of work
- Something you are **genuinely interested** in

Suggestions:
| Type | Ideas |
|---|---|
| Communication | presentation on a topic you know; post(s) explaining a complex topic to a general audience; proposal or pitch for an idea; personal/professional bio with supporting materials |
| Research | summary of an emerging technology or trend; analyse a dataset and make recommendations; compare products/services/approaches and recommend one; investigate a historical event and present it engagingly |
| Creative | outline a short story with developed characters and plot; simple website structure with content for key pages; concept for a product, service or experience |
| Learning | structured learning plan for a skill; resource collection on a topic; tutorial or guide for a process you know well; study materials for a topic you want to master |

## Step 2: Project vision and goals
- Share the idea with Claude and **invite Claude to ask you questions** until you have a solid vision.
- Get clear on:
  - what **success** looks like
  - what would make the project particularly **valuable or meaningful** to you

## Step 3: Task breakdown and delegation analysis
1. With Claude, identify the **major tasks** needed.
2. For **each task, one at a time**, discuss:
   - What specific **skills, knowledge or AI capabilities** are needed?
   - Which parts benefit from **uniquely human strengths**?
   - Which parts could use **AI capabilities** well?
   - Where would **collaboration** have the most impact?
3. Have a **genuine conversation**: challenge assumptions, ask for clarification, stay open to unexpected insights.
4. Produce a **project plan** with major tasks and delegation decisions.
5. **Save the plan**; you return to it in lesson 11 (Description-Discernment loop) and lesson 12 (Diligence).

### Diagram: the course project across lessons
```
 07 PLAN                  11 EXECUTE                       12 DISCLOSE
 (Delegation)       ──►   (Description ⇄ Discernment) ──►  (Diligence)

```

## How this maps to the Delegation components
- Step 2 = **Problem Awareness** (vision, success criteria)
- Step 3 questions on AI capabilities = **Platform Awareness**
- Step 3 decisions = **Task Delegation**


## What comes next
Description: techniques for defining what you want, guiding how the AI approaches the task, and specifying how it should interact with you, which is how you will execute this plan.
