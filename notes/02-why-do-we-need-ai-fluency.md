# 02. Why do we need AI Fluency?

Video: ~6 min

## Learning objectives
- Understand what AI Fluency means and why it matters in a fast-changing landscape
- Recognise the three ways we collaborate with AI: **Automation, Augmentation, Agency**

## The problem: access is not fluency
- AI is reshaping how we communicate, create, learn and solve problems, at work and in personal life. Many people now have versatile assistants for writing, brainstorming, research, decision making and more.
- But **having powerful systems does not mean we know how to make the most of them, or how to use them responsibly.**
- Typical moments that reveal the gap between *access* and *fluency*:
  - getting an **unexpected response** and not knowing how to proceed
  - **struggling to explain** exactly what you need and leaving frustrated
  - wondering whether the **information you share is properly protected**
- The question the course asks: how do we move beyond "a few prompt tricks" to a thoughtful, responsible approach that keeps working as AI evolves?

## What AI Fluency is
- **Not** being a technical expert, and **not** memorising "the 10 best prompts" for this month's trending task.
- **Is** a collection of **practical skills, knowledge, insights and values** that reinforce each other and **adapt as technology changes**.
- At its heart: interacting with AI in ways that are **effective, efficient, ethical and safe**. Unpacked:
  - **maximise** what you get from AI interactions
  - **without wasting** time and energy
  - in an **honest and responsible** way
  - that **protects the privacy and security** of yourself and others

## The three modes of engaging with AI
From the presenters' research and personal experience. Understanding them shows why fluency must go beyond prompt engineering.

### 1. Automation
- AI **completes a specific task based on your instructions**. You define what is needed, AI executes.
- Examples: summarise a document, draft an email, create an image, plan a trip itinerary.
- **Works well** when you have a **clear outcome in mind**.
- **Harder** when you are not yet sure what you are looking for.

### 2. Augmentation
- You and AI **collaborate and complete the task together**. AI is not a machine automating a task but a **creative thinking and problem-solving partner**.
- Examples:
  - stuck developing a story character: bounce ideas back and forth, elaborate backstories, experiment with dialogue, refine the character
  - working through a hard **architectural problem** in an app you are building
  - formulating your thoughts on a complex research topic
- AI **does not do the work for you; it helps you do your work better**.
- **Works best** when solutions are not straightforward and you need **space to explore and experiment**.

### 3. Agency
- AI **works independently on your behalf**.
- Examples: an assistant that categorises incoming email by topic or urgency and starts drafting replies to the urgent ones; an ever-changing interactive experience for website visitors; a dynamically interactive character in a game.
- Key idea: rather than defining specific actions, **you establish the AI's knowledge and behaviour patterns**.
- Metaphor: you become **less like a scriptwriter giving exact directions and more like a director setting a vision**.

### How the modes relate
- **None is inherently better**; they serve different purposes and excel in different situations.
- You might use **all three in one project**.
- Many people **start with automation**, but the presenters found that **augmentation and agency** take most advantage of AI's unique capabilities and **often lead to the most creative and effective solutions**.

## Bigger idea: AI is not "just a tool"
- AI can act as a **tool**, a **medium**, a **partner or co-creator**, and sometimes all at once.
- That shift from tool to collaborator gives technology a **new role** in creative and problem-solving work, which means **we have new roles too** and need new skills.
- Those skills are what the AI Fluency Framework describes: **four areas of competence** (the 4Ds) apply whichever mode you use.
