# 10. A closer look at Discernment

Video: ~5 min

## Learning objectives
- Evaluate AI outputs and processes thoughtfully
- Build critical-thinking skills for AI interactions
- Identify and address quality concerns

## What Discernment is
- Evaluating AI **outputs, processes and behaviours**: your **quality control system** for AI collaboration.
- The **flip side of Description**: Description communicates what you want; Discernment decides **whether what you got actually meets your needs**.
- It helps you:
  - tell when outputs are **valuable vs. problematic**
  - recognise **strengths and limitations**
  - decide when outputs are **ready to use or need more work**
- Requires **two things**:
  1. **Domain expertise**: knowing enough to judge quality
  2. **Understanding how AI systems work**, including their typical shortcomings
- Even the most advanced systems make **reasoning errors, factual mistakes, or act unexpectedly**. Your discernment is an **essential safeguard**.

## 1. Product Discernment: the output
*The ability to judge the accuracy and value of AI-created output.*

Ask:
- Is it **factually accurate**?
- Is it **appropriate for my audience and purpose**?
- Is it **coherent and well structured**?
- Does it **meet my requirements**?
- Does it **add value / solve the problem** I intended?

(Page summary: accuracy, appropriateness, coherence, relevance.)

## 2. Process Discernment: how the AI got there
*The ability to judge the quality and effectiveness of the AI's process.*

Watch for:
- **logical errors**
- **lapses in attention**
- **inappropriate steps**
- **getting stuck** on one small detail or interpretation and failing to consider alternatives
- **circular reasoning**

Example: you are expanding one of **five outline options** the AI offered. After several rounds of ideation you notice **elements of ideas you rejected are being reinserted**. Spotting that is process discernment.

- Ensures you and the AI **stay in sync** and the AI is guided toward your vision of success.
- **Especially important for complex tasks** where the correct answer is not obvious; there, **trust in the process is everything**.

### Diagram: process vs. performance
```
 PROCESS      = the work the AI is doing      (reasoning, steps, focus, loops)
 PERFORMANCE  = how it works WITH YOU          (communication, length, responsiveness)
```

## 3. Performance Discernment: how the AI behaves with you
*The ability to judge the quality of the human-AI interaction.*

- Subtle distinction: **process = the work the AI is doing; performance = how well it interacts with you while doing it.**
- Ask:
  - Is there a **better way for it to communicate** with me for ease and productivity?
  - Is it giving the information I need **in a helpful way**?
  - Does it **respond well to feedback and direction**?
  - Is the interaction **efficient or unnecessarily complex**?
- Example: is it **asking too many questions** when you need concise answers, or **too brief** when you need comprehensive information?

## Discernment does not end with evaluation: give feedback
Effective feedback when you find a problem:
1. **Specify what** the problem is
2. **Explain why** it is a problem
3. Give **concrete suggestions** for improvement
4. **Revise your instructions or examples**

- When discernment flags a problem, **better description is often the solution**.
- But not always: sometimes you must **rethink Delegation**, because you are using the **wrong tool** or approaching the problem the **wrong way entirely**.

### Diagram: what to do when discernment finds a problem
```
 DISCERNMENT flags a problem
        │
        ├──► usually: improve DESCRIPTION
        │        say what is wrong ▸ why ▸ suggest a fix ▸ revise prompt/examples
        │
        └──► sometimes: rethink DELEGATION
                 wrong tool, or wrong approach to the problem
```

## Recap
| Description (mirror) | Discernment |
|---|---|
| Product: what I want | Product: is the output good? |
| Process: how to approach it | Process: was the approach sound? |
| Performance: how to behave | Performance: was the interaction effective? |

- Description + Discernment = a **continuous loop of instruction and evaluation that drives quality**.
- Discernment keeps AI collaboration **guided by human judgment**; even the most advanced systems benefit from human oversight.

## Exercise: Expert Discernment in your domain
1. Return to the topic you know well from lesson 3's "Explore something you love" exercise.
2. In a new chat, ask Claude for **three different explanations or analyses** of one aspect (e.g. three explanations of depth of field in photography; three analyses of fermentation in cooking; three perspectives on a historical event).
3. Evaluate each:
   - **Product**: most accurate? any errors or misconceptions? right level of detail for a learner?
   - **Process**: logical reasoning? gaps? sensible connections between concepts?
   - **Performance**: attentive to your question and responsive to feedback? terminology appropriate? how do tone and style affect clarity?
4. Feedback: tell Claude **specifically why** the strongest is effective and what makes the weakest problematic; co-create an improved version.
