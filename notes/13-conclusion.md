## The 4Ds, each with its three components
| Competency | What it is | Three components |
|---|---|---|
| **Delegation** | deciding what work is done by/with AI vs. by humans alone | **Problem** awareness · **Platform** awareness · **Task** delegation |
| **Description** | how we communicate with AI | **Product** · **Process** · **Performance** description |
| **Discernment** | critically evaluating AI outputs and behaviours | **Product** · **Process** · **Performance** discernment |
| **Diligence** | making AI interactions responsible | **Creation** · **Transparency** · **Deployment** diligence |

### Diagram: the whole framework on one page
```
               AI FLUENCY = effective · efficient · ethical · safe
                                    │
     ┌──────────────────┬───────────┴────────┬────────────────────┐
 DELEGATION        DESCRIPTION          DISCERNMENT          DILIGENCE
 ▸ Problem         ▸ Product            ▸ Product            ▸ Creation
 ▸ Platform        ▸ Process            ▸ Process            ▸ Transparency
 ▸ Task            ▸ Performance        ▸ Performance        ▸ Deployment
     └──────────────────┴───────────┬────────┴────────────────────┘
                     used in every mode of working with AI:
                    AUTOMATION · AUGMENTATION · AGENCY
```

These apply across all **three modes** of interaction:
- **Automation**: AI performs specific tasks following your instructions
- **Augmentation**: you and AI collaborate as thinking partners
- **Agency**: AI is configured to act independently on your behalf

## Essential insights to carry forward
- **Delegation**: your **expertise and judgment remain the foundation** of effective AI use. The most powerful outcomes come from spotting where you and AI can **build on each other's strengths through iterative collaboration**, creating outcomes **neither could achieve alone**.
- **Description**: clear communication **bridges your intentions and AI capabilities**. Often the most effective instructions are **not elaborate prompts but thoughtful conversations** that provide context, examples and feedback.
- **Discernment**: critical evaluation of AI output is a **non-negotiable responsibility**. It protects against AI limitations and enables results neither side could easily create alone.
- **Diligence**: **you remain accountable** for what you do with AI and its impact on others. **Transparency builds trust and integrity.**

## On developing fluency
- These competencies are **not mastered overnight**; they grow through **practice**: delegate, describe, discern, apply diligence.
- **The goal is not perfection**; it is continually developing **awareness, commitment and an intentional approach**.
- The framework is designed to apply across the broad range of generative AI assistants and to **stay relevant as AI evolves**, but it is up to us to keep applying it.

## Closing message
- AI systems are powerful but **not silver bullets or magical solutions**. They are **only as useful and safe as we enable them to be**.
- So:
  - **invest in your own expertise** and **stay aware of technological change** so you can delegate appropriately
  - **practise description and discernment**, the back-and-forth that turns AI from automation machines into **genuine thinking partners**
  - **take responsibility** for how you work with AI and what you create together
- **Be effective and efficient. Be ethical and safe.**

## Key takeaways (lesson page)
- Fluency develops through **intentional practice** of the four competencies
- Delegation: expertise and judgment remain the foundation
- Description: clear communication bridges intentions and AI capability
- Discernment: thoughtful, critical evaluation to work within the system's constraints
- Diligence: accountability, transparency, responsibility
- The most powerful outcomes come when humans and AI **build on each other's strengths**
- The framework is built to **remain relevant** as AI evolves
